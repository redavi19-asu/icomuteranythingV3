BoxRoute USA Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026. This policy covers the BoxRoute USA iPhone app from I Computer Anything.

Shipping labels and routes

The app processes camera scans on your iPhone. Delivery addresses, package records, saved label photos, and route progress are stored by the app on your device. BoxRoute has no account system and sends no copies of your saved labels or route history to a BoxRoute server.

Location and maps

With permission, the app uses your location to identify your delivery area, calculate directions, and provide package reminders. Mail Companion can use location while you’re delivering, including when the app is in the background while monitoring is active. You can turn Mail Companion off in the app or revoke location access in iPhone Settings.

BoxRoute uses Apple location and mapping services to look up addresses and calculate directions. If you open a route in Apple Maps or Google Maps, BoxRoute passes the applicable destinations to that service, which handles them under its own privacy policy.

Subscriptions and support

Apple processes subscription purchases. BoxRoute checks subscription status through StoreKit and does not receive your payment card details. If you contact support, we use the information you send to respond to your request.

Your choices

Use the app’s clear actions to remove delivery records or delete the app to remove its local data. You can change camera, location, and notification permissions in iPhone Settings.

For privacy questions, contact I Computer Anything support.
